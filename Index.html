About us

Clear answers for Milton homeowners.

Mouse Free Milton was created for one purpose: to give homeowners honest, practical information about mice. Rodent problems usually start quietly. Our guides help you notice them early, understand the health and property risks and take the right next step.

We write for real households. That means explaining how mice move through older foundations, why garages and crawl spaces matter and how to clean contamination without putting your family at risk.
